# System Transparency 1.1.7

## Fixes

- Restored the original tray context menu and Settings hierarchy instead of the new top-level Opacity/Shortcuts panel introduced in 1.1.6.
- Custom Alpha remains under `Setting > Preset > Custom Alpha...`, with its vertical slider, live preview, numeric value, and OK/Cancel controls.
- Hotkeys remain under `Setting > Hotkeys...`. Click the shortcut combination text itself to record a new combination; no separate Record button is required.
- Removed the extra More menu and Back navigation. Existing settings, presets, language options, updates, logs, and uninstall actions are available in their original locations.
- Retained immediate saving of completed shortcut recordings, Apply/Restore conflict checks, keyboard navigation, and Escape cancellation.
- Updated English and Korean documentation and interaction checks for the restored menu flow.

## Usage

Right-click the tray icon and open Setting. Use Preset > Custom Alpha to adjust opacity, or Hotkeys to click and record a shortcut. Hold modifier keys and middle-click for Apply/Restore or scroll for Adjust. Click the combination again or press Escape to cancel recording.
